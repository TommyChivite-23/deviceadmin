How a request flows

  1. Route receives the click and calls a service (e.g. PhoneService.reboot).
  2. Service loads the device from its repository and asks the connector interface to act, wrapped in retry-with-backoff.
  3. Connector (mock here, real elsewhere) returns data or raises a typed error — timeout, rate limit, bad request, auth failure.
  4. The service turns the outcome into an audit-log entry and a user-facing message; the database is only updated when the device accepted the change.
